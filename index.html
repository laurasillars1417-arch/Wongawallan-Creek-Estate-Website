Your quiet escape

Wongawallan Creek Estate is a peaceful, flat, open property beside a clear freshwater creek. There's plenty of room to spread out, with old shade trees and hills behind you. The creek has both deep and shallow sections, which makes it great for families to swim and splash.

Keep an eye out for wildlife. The creek is home to platypus and river bass, and guests regularly spot kingfishers, black cockatoos and king parrots. Bring your binoculars and floaties.

You're a short walk from Stanley's barn restaurant and the Gold Coast Car Museum. Tamborine Mountain, the theme parks and the beaches are all an easy drive.
